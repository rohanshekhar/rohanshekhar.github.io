Personal rclone client

This is a private Google Cloud project used by me to connect rclone, an open-source file sync tool, to their own Google Drive account. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This application is used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive. Files and account information accessed through the Google Drive API are transferred directly between the owner’s computer and Google.

No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties. The application’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke this application’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
